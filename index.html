How the math works

Percentage Formulas

These formulas cover the most common searches for percent calculator, percentage increase calculator, discount calculator, tip calculator, tax calculator, and markup calculator.

Percentage of a number

Formula: result = percentage / 100 x value

To find 15% of 240, calculate 0.15 x 240 = 36.

What percent is one number of another?

Formula: percentage = part / whole x 100

If 45 out of 180 orders are returned, 45 / 180 x 100 = 25%.

Percentage increase or decrease

Formula: change = (new - original) / original x 100

If a price rises from 80 to 92, the increase is 15%.

Discount sale price

Formula: sale price = original price x (1 - discount / 100)

For $50 at 30% off, the sale price is $35 and the savings are $15.

FAQ

Percentage Calculator Questions

How do you calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100, then multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 150 is 0.20 x 150 = 30.

How do you calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. Positive results are increases; negative results are decreases.

How do you calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by discount percent / 100 to find the savings. Subtract the savings from the original price to find the sale price.

Does this calculator store my numbers?

Recent calculations are stored only in your browser on your device. They are not sent to a server by this app.
